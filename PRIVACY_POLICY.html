Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Pomowriter
Effective Date: March 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pomowriter. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Pomowriter is a desktop application designed to assist with writing and project management. To provide seamless data synchronization and backup features, our app requests access to your Google Drive account.

  • Google Drive Data: We request permissions to upload, download, and view specific file and folder lists within your Google Drive.
  • Purpose: This access is used strictly for syncing and backing up your writing projects across your devices. We do not read, modify, or delete any files in your Google Drive that were not created or managed by Pomowriter.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Pomowriter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

  • No External Servers: Pomowriter operates locally on your device and interacts directly with Google Drive. We do not store your Google Drive data, writing content, or personal information on any external or third-party servers.
  • No Data Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or Google Drive data to outside parties under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since Pomowriter does not store your data on our servers, you have full control over your data. You can delete your synced files directly from your Google Drive at any time. You can also revoke Pomowriter's access to your Google account through your Google Account Security settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
